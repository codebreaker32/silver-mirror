SkinKey · Design system v1.1 · 1 Nov 2026

Clay & Ink, with four parts from Deep Teal

Sand-paper neutrals and one deep blue-green ink. Newsreader sets titles, section heads, the verdict line and the numbered reasons; Commissioner sets the interface. Everything on this page is drawn from tokens/tokens.css: no hex value appears in the component CSS. Phone frames are 390 × 844, rendered once in light and once in dark.

Expo / React Native (Android-first, Material 3 behaviour for share, back, permissions, sheets and snackbars) plus a small web app (results and fallback page, pharmacist QR page, landing) reading the same tokens as CSS variables.

Principles

  1. A calm friend who shows their working. Every claim names its source: the scan date, the product data, the weather.
  2. Reasons before verdicts. Reasons out-size the verdict label and are numbered in serif.
  3. Privacy you can see. What we keep before the camera; the YouCam deletion as its own step.
  4. Honest UNKNOWN. A designed state with a next step and the real catalogue count.
  5. Fit, not safety. CAUTION is ochre, never red, never the whole screen. The medicine gate is one quiet line.

Type scale

Newsreader (variable, optical size follows font size) for display, title, section heads, the verdict line and the reason line; Commissioner for everything you tap or scan. Changelog

Where the system came from. Full detail in README.md and tokens/contrast-report.md.

Grafts from Deep Teal (v1.0)

  1. Dispensing-label ledger. "Where this comes from" with small-caps keys SKIN / PRODUCT / WEATHER / LIMITS, heavier ink rules top and bottom, hairline rows. Reused for the /profile band list.
  2. Tint in the verdict head only. Reasons, ledger and buttons sit on the plain surface.
  3. Neutral-ink band meters. Horizontal 3-segment meter, full-opacity empty outlines, never the accent.
  4. Pharmacist at a counter for the medicine gate, now with one bottle on the counter. The mortar and pestle is retired.
  5. Judge fixes. Verdict label 15 / 600 with a 24dp icon; fact 16dp in text colour; dark primary #86A4A7 / #0C171A (6.84:1); 12dp floor; 48dp targets; four silhouettes; grain static, decorative-only, off in dark.

Critique round 2 (v1.1)

  1. Verdict head loses the inset stripe; the sentence is Newsreader 20/26; UNKNOWN moves to warm paper with a dashed edge; dark FITS / CAUTION fills desaturated.
  2. /check is a pushed detail: no bottom nav, one primary plus a 48dp tertiary, one destination line, bar lifts only when content scrolls under it.
  3. Medicine gate is a bare serif line under the 32dp pharmacist, a third of the way down. No box.
  4. Eyebrows removed. Caps survive only on ledger keys and the verdict label; section heads are sentence-case Newsreader 18.
  5. New parts: band list, skin-map egg face with "Your left / Your right", vignette camera, neutral try-on plate, left-aligned empty state, share-sheet hero on home, Check / Aisle / Profile nav.
  6. Accessibility: inverse and overlay focus rings, nav selection by weight + stroke, single hidden live region, no hitSlop-only targets, minHeight everywhere, rem type on the web, forced-colours rules.
