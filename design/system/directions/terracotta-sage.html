SkinKey design directions · C of 3 · slug terracotta-sage

Indian Earth:
terracotta on khadi, with sage.

Unbleached-cotton neutrals, one fired-clay accent used once per screen, and a sage that only ever means "fits" or "done". The display face is Eczar, a Rosetta typeface by Indian designer Vaibhav Singh, which carries calligraphic stress from Devanagari into a confident Latin. The text face is Anek Latin by Ek Type: plain enough for reasons and chips, warm in its terminals. The one ornament is the kantha running stitch, a dashed rule that marks where provenance begins.

/check · CAUTION

Reason before label weight: the reading (moment phrasing) is set in semibold, the product fact follows in plain words, and the band chip ties it back to /profile. The only terracotta on the screen is the one action that leaves the app, and its destination is printed directly under it.

Radius, elevation, motion

Radius
4 / 8 / 12 / 18, pill only for the scan-date chip. Cards and verdict panels 18, buttons and inputs 12, chips 8-10.
Elevation
Tonal layering: bg, surface, raised, each with a 1dp hairline. Only the bottom action sheet and modal sheets cast a shadow, tinted khadi-900. In dark mode shadows are dropped and raised surfaces get lighter.
Ornament
Kantha running stitch: 1.5dp dashed rule, 6 on / 5 off, in the panel's border colour. Used for section seams and before provenance. Nothing else is decorative.
Motion
"Settling clay". Standard 220ms, emphasis 320ms, cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.7, 0.2, 1). The verdict panel rises 8dp and stops, reasons follow at 60ms. No springs, no loops. Press: scale 0.98, 120ms.
Reduce motion
Translations and staggers removed; 150ms opacity crossfade only. Processing steps become static ticks.
Fonts
@expo-google-fonts/eczar 0.4.1 and @expo-google-fonts/anek-latin 0.4.2 (both checked on npm). Load Eczar_500Medium, Eczar_600SemiBold, AnekLatin_400Regular, _500Medium, _600SemiBold, _700Bold.
