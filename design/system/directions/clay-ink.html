Clay & Ink

Printed matter, not a beauty counter. Warm sand paper, one deep blue-green ink, and a bookish serif that sets the reasons like a well-made label. The verdict is a small stamp; the reasons are the story. Calm enough to read in a store aisle, and confident enough for someone who already pays for serums.
