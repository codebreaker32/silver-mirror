## How the values got here

- v2.0 Silver Mirror (2026-11-02). Source: design/reference/silver-mirror-moodboard.md (Aman's moodboard), critique-current.md and dribbble-patterns.md.
- Neutrals are one cool ramp with a faint lavender cast (hue about 250 degrees, saturation under 12%): pearl (light surfaces), silver (mid greys, text levels), graphite (dark surfaces). No warm/cool mixing inside a theme.
- Primary = deep slate #3B3F52 pill with pearl label (9.6:1). Dark primary is inverted: pearl #F6F5F8 pill with slate #2F3243 label (10.9:1), so the one action per screen is the brightest object in a graphite room.
- UNKNOWN head is pearl/graphite neutral with a dashed slate edge, not a slate fill, so it can never be mistaken for the slate primary pill (critique S10 / 5.3). FITS = muted sage, CAUTION = soft amber (hue 37-39 degrees in every role), MEDICINE = ink outline only.
- Greige #CFC4BB is a fill only (1.6:1 on pearl): Demo-reading badge, focus tiles. Never text, never a meaningful edge.
- Dark theme is graphite #121319 with surfaces stepping lighter (#1A1B23, #20212A), a lavender-silver sheen gradient on hero zones and a 1dp top highlight on raised cards. No shadows in dark; elevation is tone.
- Type: Noto Serif Display (high-contrast, has the rupee sign) for the wordmark, display and title only, never below 20dp. Geist (has the rupee sign, tabular figures) for every control, label, reason and body line.
- Paper grain retired (opacity 0 in both modes); depth now comes from soft slate-tinted offset shadows in light and tone steps in dark.
- Icon stroke 1.5 (moodboard thin line); active tab 2.0 so selection is stroke + weight + pill, never colour alone.
- 2.1.0 (showcase delta, design/reference/showcase-delta.md section 2): mist page bg #EFECF2 with white cards and a violet-tinted shadow (#3A3360); text-subtle #5E5B6B keeps 4.5:1 on the darker mist wells; on-icon-disc iris #5A5B9F (light) / lavender.300 (dark); every heading moves to Geist 600; bottom nav loses its pill (filled glyph + weight + colour carry selection); focus tiles leave greige for white tiles with lavender discs.
- 2.2.0 (design/reference/colors-reference.png): light is re-mapped to the colors reference. Page = vertical gradient page-top #F8F1F9 -> page-bottom #EDDCF3 (bg #F3E7F7 is the solid mid tone) with a blush radial glow top-left; cards are glass #FCF8FE with a 1dp white hairline at 70% and a plum-tinted shadow (#5A3F6B); primary is plum #67506F (the reference #776083 darkened so the white label keeps the 7:1 primary-pill lint), pressed #574260; pastel tiles tile-pink #F8D9DF, tile-lilac #EADEEA, tile-lavender #DCCFE9; accent-pink #F7B3CC is a fill only. Text moves to the plum-grey mauve ramp (text #241B29, muted #54475B, subtle #67596E, all AA on every page stop, glass and the sunken well). Icon discs are a pinker lilac #F3DDEC with a plum-violet line #6A4673. Verdict roles keep sage / amber; UNKNOWN and MEDICINE move onto the blush neutrals. Sheen, liquid and mark stay as they were (the welcome backdrop is finished).
- 2.2.1 (review fixes): the light sheen leaves grey for the palette (start #FDF8FD -> lavender #F3E6F5 -> blush #F6DDEB on the 'silver' role -> end #EEDFF2), so face-frame, empty-state and try-on plates read pink-lavender; the mist ramp is retired (nothing in light fed from it any more); secondary-translucent becomes a blush glass rgba(252, 240, 248, 0.70); welcome-wash lays #F6DDEB at 30% over the welcome photo.
- 2.3.0 (Silver & graphite, Aman 2 Nov): the pink-lavender 2.2 light theme is retired as feminine-coded and glassy. New mirror ramp: page #ECEDF0, surface #F8F8FA, well #E1E3E8 (tiles #DADDE3 / #E6E7EB), text #15161A, muted #565964, subtle #5F626C (4.5:1 on all three tones), border-strong #7A7E89, primary #1E2026 / pressed #34363E with white labels, steel #44607A for selection, links and focus. Elevation 1 and 2 cast no shadow and carry no border; only elevation 3 (sheets, dialogs) floats. Verdict heads sit on the surface tone; the verdict reads by glyph + word + tinted disc; UNKNOWN is a solid well, no dashed edge. One radius set: surfaces 16, thumbnails 12, buttons full. Small type has normal tracking.
- 2.4.0 (Lavender-grey & charcoal, Aman 2 Nov, design/reference/experience-reference.png): the mirror ramp keeps its names but takes a quiet lavender cast. Page #EDECF2, surface #F7F6FA, well #E4E2EB, product-art plate #E9E7F0 (mirror.150), text #1B1A22, muted #575566, subtle #625F70 (4.5:1 on every tone), border-strong #7D7A8A, primary = selection = link = focus #3A3546 (pressed #2C2836) with white labels, icon glyphs #4A4658. UNKNOWN reads as well + ink. Motion: press 150ms, toast 180ms, sheet 280ms, skeleton pulse 1.2s (stops under reduced motion).
