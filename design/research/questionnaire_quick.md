# SkinKey: Quick Interview Questionnaire (about 10 min per person)

**Who to ask:** people aged 20–35 who bought a skincare product online (Amazon, Flipkart or Nykaa) in the last 3 months. Aim for a mix of men and women, skin types and budgets. Include at least one person who has never read an ingredient list and one person from a Tier 2 or Tier 3 city if you can.

**Screener (ask both; continue only if the answer to 1 is yes):**
1. "Have you bought any skincare online in the last 3 months?" (yes / no)
2. "Roughly how old are you: under 20, 20–35, or over 35?"

**Consent (read aloud):**
"We're students building a skincare shopping app for a hackathon. I'll ask a few questions for about 10 minutes and take written notes only. No photos or recordings. You can skip any question or stop anytime. Is that okay?"

---

## Part A: Warm-up (1 min)
1. What skincare do you use on a normal day? (Just list the products.)
2. How would you describe your skin, in your own words? *Probe: "What makes you say that?" (e.g. shine by noon, or something they saw in a reel)*

## Part B: Last purchase (3 min)
3. Tell me about the last skincare product you bought online. What was it, and which app or site did you use?
4. How did you first hear about it? (reel, friend, ad, search, dermatologist…)
5. Before buying, how did you decide it would suit **your** skin? *Probe: what did you look at, e.g. reviews, ingredients, ratings, "for oily skin" labels?*
6. Was anything confusing or worrying at that point? *Probe: ingredient names, too many options, fake reviews, price.*
7. After using it, how did it go? *Probe: are you still using it? Did you return it, give it away, or stop using it? Why? If it didn't work: "What did you do with it afterwards?"*

## Part C: Ingredients and trust (2 min)
8. Are there any ingredients you look for or avoid? How did you learn about them?
9. Whose advice do you trust most when choosing skincare, and why?
10. How many skincare products do you own right now that you **don't** use? (A rough number is fine.)

## Part D: Face scan attitude (1 min)
11. Have you ever used an app or website that scans your face for skin or makeup? What did you think of it?
12. What would make you comfortable, or uncomfortable, letting an app analyse a selfie of your skin? *Probe: "Would it change anything if the photo was deleted right after the scan and only the readings were kept?"*

## Part E: Concept reaction (2 min)
Read this aloud word for word without selling it: *"Imagine an app where you scan your face once. Later, when you're looking at a product on Amazon or Flipkart, you tap Share, choose this app, and it tells you whether that product fits your skin, with reasons, and shows better matches if it doesn't."*

13. What's your first reaction?
14. When would you actually use this, if ever?
15. What would make you **not** trust its answer?
16. If it said **"Unknown, we don't have enough info about this product"**, how would you feel about that? *Probe: "What if it then asked you to snap a photo of the ingredient list?"*
16b. Nykaa and Flipkart already have skin scans in their apps. Have you tried them? Why would you, or wouldn't you, use something like this instead?

## Part F: Wrap-up (1 min)
17. Is there anything about buying skincare online that frustrates you that we haven't talked about?
18. Can we quote something you said (no name or face) in our demo video? (yes / no)

Thank them.

---

## Optional: 5-minute share-flow test (if they have the Amazon or Flipkart app)

1. "Open any skincare product in your Amazon or Flipkart app."
2. "Show me how you'd send this product to another app." *Observe:* do they find **Share**? How long does it take? Where do they hesitate?
3. Show a sketch or mock of the verdict screen: **"CAUTION: Contains heavy oils. Your oiliness reading: High (scan 1 Nov)"** with two buttons, **"Back to product"** and **"Find better matches"**.
4. Ask:
   - "In your own words, what is this screen telling you?"
   - "What do you expect happens if you tap 'Find better matches'?"
   - "What does 'Your oiliness reading: High' mean to you?"
   - "Would you trust this? Why or why not?"
   - "What's missing?"

**Don't help or explain during the test.** Note where they get stuck.

**Afterwards, a 15-minute debrief:** write each observation on a sticky note or a line of text, group similar ones, and name each group. Then send me the grid. I'll synthesize it and adjust the designs.

### Interviewer rules
- Ask about **what they actually did**, not what they "would" do.
- Don't mention SkinKey's features until Part E.
- Use neutral prompts like "Tell me more", "Why?", "What happened next?"
- Never say "Wouldn't it be great if…".
- Silence is fine. Let them fill it.
